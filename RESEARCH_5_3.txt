APEX SCALP GOD 5.3 — DEEP MARKET RESEARCH NOTES

PUBLIC RESEARCH BASIS
1. Order-flow imbalance / market depth: Cont, Kukanov & Stoikov show short-horizon price changes are closely linked to order-flow imbalance, with impact depending on depth. 5.3 therefore treats persistent one-way pressure as a continuation warning rather than blindly fading it.
2. Crypto reversal is state-dependent: literature finds short-term reversal/liquidity-provision effects, but the most liquid major coins can display momentum and transaction costs can erase a gross edge. 5.3 therefore keeps explicit cost and spread-stress gates.
3. Bitcoin intraday structure matters: high-frequency work documents intraday variation in volume, volatility and liquidity. 5.3 records a compact live micro-tape and evaluates current spread against the symbol's recent spread distribution.
4. Liquidation cascades: 2026 studies of perpetual-futures stress events show sharp futures-led cascades, extreme spread expansion, hidden liquidity and execution-risk distortions. 5.3 adds a CASCADE / NEWS IMPULSE state and blocks inverse fades while persistent impact is active.
5. Gold is news-state dependent: high-frequency gold research finds FOMC and macro surprises can drive price/volatility adjustment for more than five minutes; US futures and the London/New York overlap are central to price discovery. 5.3 treats critical news + persistent impulse as a continuation regime.
6. USDJPY and macro news: high-frequency research finds Japanese and US macro announcements can materially change USD/JPY trading intensity and volatility, with many effects concentrated in the first 30 minutes. This supports event-state-aware rather than static inverse rules.
7. Jumps matter: macro-related intraday jumps are larger, more persistent and have stronger microstructure effects than ordinary moves. 5.3 computes a robust OHLC jump proxy and volatility-burst proxy to distinguish ordinary exhaustion from information shocks.
8. Backtest overfitting: research on PBO/Deflated Sharpe and newer comparisons of validation schemes warns that ordinary walk-forward testing can still select false edges under non-stationarity. 5.3 adds chronological holdout, effective-sample adjustment and a block-dependence expectancy floor before promotion.
