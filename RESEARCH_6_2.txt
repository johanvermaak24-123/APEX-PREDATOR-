WHAT CHANGED
- State-first inference: microstructure state sets a prior before path/flow evidence is credited.
- Epistemic uncertainty tax: small samples, unknown costs, unstable holdouts, evidence conflict and drift reduce decision probability.
- Recency-decayed analog memory: older crypto paths decay faster than recent paths; stale evidence cannot dominate forever.
- Flow/depth toxicity proxy: available OHLC/tick-volume pressure is judged relative to absorption/rejection and liquidity fragility. This is explicitly NOT direct L2 depth.
- Alpha retirement: recent negative empirical expectancy or the existing loss firewall can retire an edge rather than keep averaging into it.
- Edge half-life: the UI reports a short validity horizon by state so old scalp signals are not treated as timeless.
- Hydra execution veto: statistically promoted setups still cannot execute if toxicity/fragility state is unsafe.
