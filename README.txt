FORENSIC LIMITATION
This remains a browser-only static app. Historical News Evidence becomes genuine as the app accumulates archived decisions. It does NOT contain a preloaded institutional historical economic-event database (actual/forecast/revision/yields/DXY reaction history). A server/database and dedicated historical event data source are required for that next tier.

IMPORTANT
No trading system can guarantee profits or a fixed accuracy. “Model Score” is an evidence-strength heuristic, not a calibrated probability until enough independent resolved outcomes exist.
