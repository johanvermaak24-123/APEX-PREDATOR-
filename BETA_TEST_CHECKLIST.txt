APEX 7.2.0 — BETA USER TEST CHECKLIST
======================================

Run these tests before any commercial release.

1. FRESH LOAD
   Refresh the page. It must open in SIMPLE MODE and the button must say
   SHOW ADVANCED DETAILS. Technical cards must not be visible.

2. SINGLE MARKET — WAIT
   Scan a market that returns WAIT. The main answer must say WAIT — DO NOT ENTER.
   Entry, Stop Loss and Take Profit must remain blank. No large BUY/SELL thesis
   should appear on the normal screen.

3. SINGLE MARKET — ENTER
   If/when a fully authorized signal appears, verify the big answer says exactly
   ENTER BUY NOW or ENTER SELL NOW and that Entry, Stop Loss and Take Profit are
   populated and directionally correct.

4. OPEN TRADE MANAGEMENT
   Tap I ENTERED THIS TRADE. On later scans the main trade line must say HOLD,
   TAKE PROFIT NOW or EXIT NOW. It must never create a second ENTER instruction
   for the already-open trade.

5. FIND A TRADE — NOTHING READY
   Scan all markets. If no market qualifies, the screen must say
   WAIT — NO TRADE READY. It must not show a tempting closest BUY/SELL candidate.

6. FIND A TRADE — READY
   If a market qualifies, only one ready trade should be shown in Consumer Mode,
   with exact market, Entry, Stop Loss and Take Profit.

7. ADVANCED DETAILS
   Tap SHOW ADVANCED DETAILS. Research panels may appear. Refresh the page and
   confirm it returns to SIMPLE MODE automatically.

8. STALE ANSWER
   Leave an ENTER answer untouched for more than the command expiry period. It
   must change to WAIT / SCAN AGAIN and remove live entry levels.

9. MEMORY
   Refresh/update the app without clearing site data. Confirm journal/learning
   persists under Advanced Details / Settings.

10. REAL-BROKER PILOT
   Use demo or very small risk first. Record at least spread, slippage, entry fill,
   stop/target behavior and every HOLD/EXIT instruction. Do not market win-rate
   claims until there is enough independent forward evidence.
