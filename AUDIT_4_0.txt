RESEARCH BASIS IMPLEMENTED AS GUARDRAILS, NOT GUARANTEES
- Short-horizon reversal/mean-reversion can exist, especially after aggressive flow, but transaction costs can overwhelm small gross edges.
- Order-flow imbalance / liquidity pressure can matter at short horizons, but stability must be validated out of sample.
- Gold response to FOMC shocks can continue beyond the first 5 minutes, so event-driven continuation risk must block naive fading.

LIMITATIONS
- Current path engine uses 5-minute OHLC paths, so same-bar tick ordering is intentionally not inferred.
- The OHLC+tick-volume liquidity-pressure feature is a proxy, not full exchange L2 order-book data.
- Browser static QA cannot prove future profitability, broker fills, slippage, or regime stability.
