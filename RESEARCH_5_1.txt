APEX SCALP GOD 5.1 — RESEARCH BASIS

Implemented principles:
1. Short-horizon reversal can exist, especially after aggressive flow, but gross reversal edge can be smaller than transaction costs. Therefore live spread/target cost remains a hard execution gate.
2. Gold can continue adjusting for more than five minutes after monetary-policy shocks. Therefore critical supportive continuation shocks veto blind inverse fading.
3. Backtest optimization can overfit. Therefore 5.1 separates RESEARCH/SCOUT/CONFIRMED/GOD EDGE, uses sample thresholds, conservative Wilson lower confidence bounds, multi-regime evidence and live confirmation rather than promoting one strong-looking historical percentage.
4. A fixed 0-30m counter benchmark is recorded on resolved paths: +0.16 ATR target, -0.32 ATR stop, conservative same-bar collision counted as stop. This produces a comparable empirical expectancy statistic rather than treating a model score as a win probability.
5. Parent thesis remains context only. It cannot directly authorize execution in the scalp layer.

Research references reviewed for this release:
- Kitron & Wengrowicz (2026), Short-horizon mean reversion in cryptocurrency markets: reversal pervasive at 15m in crypto, but gross edge near 1.3 bp versus a 5 bp round-trip benchmark cost.
- Awartani, Hussain & Virk (2024), gold intraday response to FOMC shocks: return/volatility adjustment can persist beyond five minutes.
- Bailey, Borwein, Lopez de Prado & Zhu, Probability of Backtest Overfitting: strategy selection on large backtest searches can generate statistical mirages; out-of-sample and anti-overfit controls are required.

This research does not establish guaranteed profitability. The app must earn promotion through resolved outcomes.
